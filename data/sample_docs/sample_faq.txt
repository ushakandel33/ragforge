Company Policy FAQ

Remote Work Policy: Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week,
subject to manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10 AM to 3 PM
local time, during which employees should be reachable.

Leave Policy: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid leave per year.
Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
Sick leave is tracked separately and does not require advance notice.

Expense Reimbursement: Submit expense reports within 30 days of the
expense date. Reports must include itemized receipts for any single
expense above $25. Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days.

Equipment Policy: New employees receive a laptop and standard peripherals
within their first week. Additional equipment requests go through the IT
ticketing system and require manager sign-off for items over $200.
